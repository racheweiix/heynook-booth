Good to know

Questions before take-off

The short version: one passport, five checkpoints, one point each, a prize at the end.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Your passport opens in the browser from the link in your confirmation email. Keep it handy on your phone.

How do I collect a checkpoint?

Show your passport QR to the crew at the checkpoint. Once they scan it, the checkpoint turns to explored and +1 pt lands straight away.

Do I have to go in order?

The trajectory suggests a route, but every checkpoint counts whenever you reach it. Your passport always shows what's left.

Can a checkpoint count twice?

No. Each checkpoint is worth one point, once. Five different checkpoints get you to the launch pad.

How do I claim my prize?

When all five are explored, your passport shows Lift-off and unlocks the prize. Head to the launch pad and show the crew your screen.
