Good to know

Questions, answered

Anything else? Ask at any station. The team is there all day.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Your passport is a web page. It opens in your phone's browser from the link with your ticket.

Do I need to know about AI?

Not at all. Every station is made for a first try, and someone is on hand to walk you through it.

What is each station worth?

One point each, five in all. Each part can only be collected once, so the way to a full board is to try every station.

Can I visit the stations in any order?

Yes. Start wherever there's no queue. The part lights up on your board whenever its station scans you in.

How do I claim my prize?

Once all five parts are lit, your prize screen opens. Show it at the prize desk.
