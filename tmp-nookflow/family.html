How the passport works

Your passport lives on your phone

Every family gets a digital passport with their booking. It opens in the browser, so there is nothing to download.

  1. 01

    Open your passport

    Tap the link in your confirmation email. You will see the board, your stamps and your points so far.

  2. 02

    Play a stop, get scanned

    Bowl, fish, paint or hunt. When you finish, the stop crew scans your passport QR.

  3. 03

    Watch the stamp land

    The square turns pink and your points go up by one. Each stop stamps once.

  4. 04

    Make lines, fill the board

    Three in a row unlocks a mini prize. All eight stops enter you in the grand prize draw.

Good to know

Questions from families

Do we need to download an app?

No. The passport opens in your phone's browser from the link in your confirmation email.

Can the whole family share one passport?

Yes. Each booking comes with a passport, and families play the stops together on one phone.

Do we have to go in order?

No. Play the stops in any order. The numbers only show where each stop sits on the board.

What is the free pass?

The centre square. It is stamped from the start, so the middle row, the middle column and both diagonals each need just two more stops.

Can we play a stop twice?

Play as often as you like. The stamp and its point are counted once per passport.

Where do we collect prizes?

Show your passport at the prize booth. Mini prizes are ready as soon as a line is done, and full boards go into the grand prize draw.
