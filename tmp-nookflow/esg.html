Good to know

Questions, answered

Anything else? Ask any station team on the day.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Your passport opens in the browser from the link in your confirmation email.

Do I have to visit the stations in order?

No. Every station has its own plot, so its flower comes up whenever you get there.

Can a station count twice?

No. Each station counts once and is worth 1 pt, so the garden tops out at 5.

What if my scan doesn't show up?

Ask the station team to scan you again. The flower appears as soon as your QR is verified.

Where do I collect my prize?

Once your garden is in full bloom, show your passport at the prize desk.

Is the passport on this page real?

It's a demo. Press Tap to scan to see what happens at each station. Nothing you do here is saved.

Ready to grow your garden?

Open the demo passport and plant all five flowers yourself. It takes about a minute.
